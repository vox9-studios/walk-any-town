# One place per line. Each becomes a walkable square, centred on the place.
# Be specific: "High Street, East Grinstead, UK" works better than "East Grinstead".
#
# A line may also carry, after a | , how far the square reaches from its centre in metres
# (100 to 400, the default is 200), and a centre of your own as lat,lon. A centre of your own
# is the way to hold two streets in one square when neither sits in the middle.
#
# A field beginning with @ says where the walk should begin: you are put on the named street
# nearest that mark, facing along it. Without one you start near the middle of the square.
#
#   Market Place, Hexham, UK | 300
#   High Street, Lewes, UK | 250 | 50.8736,0.0088
#   Pulteney Bridge, Bath, UK | 250 | @51.3813,-2.3580
#
# Lines starting with # are ignored. After editing, commit: the site rebuilds itself.
